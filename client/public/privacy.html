Data Privacy & Protection

Privacy Policy & Data Handling Disclosure

This Privacy Policy describes our practices concerning the collection, usage, storage, encryption, and disclosure of user information across the Z Sport simulation and educational analytics infrastructure.

Educational Data Environment & Third-Party Gateways

We respect your personal privacy. Our platform is designed as an educational simulation. We never sell, rent, or trade your personally identifiable information to unauthorized commercial brokers. All payment interactions are delegated to external, certified third-party gateway providers (such as Flutterwave or Paystack) governed by their respective PCI-DSS compliance frameworks.

1. Information We Collect

When you interact with our platform, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, hashed passwords, telephone contact details, and country of residence.
  • Technical & Telemetry Data: IP address, device fingerprints, browser user agents, operating system details, and timestamped access logs.
  • Simulated Platform Activity: Virtual bet slips, session history, odds calculations, simulation token ledger entries, and referral attribution tags.

2. How Information is Utilized

We process collected telemetry strictly for operational, anti-fraud, and simulation maintenance purposes:

  • To initialize and secure your user dashboard session.
  • To prevent multi-accounting, automated botting, and platform abuse.
  • To evaluate algorithm load, caching efficiency, and server responsiveness.
  • To verify compliance with our 18+ age restrictions and multi-tier KYC protocols.

3. Payment Processing & Gateway Disclaimers

The platform does not store raw credit card numbers, bank PINs, or mobile money OTPs on internal servers. All transactional interactions initiated by users are redirected to certified external third-party payment gateways. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, telemetry retention, or data breaches of third-party payment services.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

Our web application utilizes persistent and session-based cookies, as well as HTML5 `localStorage`, to store authentication JWT tokens, UI theme preferences, and currency settings. You may disable cookies in your browser settings, though doing so may disable vital platform functionality such as session persistence.

5. Data Security & Retention

We employ industry-standard cryptographic protocols (including TLS 1.3 in transit and AES-256 database-level encryption) to safeguard stored credentials. While we maintain rigorous defensive protocols, no digital transmission across the public internet is 100% immune from compromise. Users assume full responsibility for maintaining the confidentiality of their passwords.

6. Rights of Access & Account Deletion

Users possess the right to request a summary of their stored account data or request permanent deletion of their profile records by submitting an authorized request to our designated privacy desk at support@zsports.live.
